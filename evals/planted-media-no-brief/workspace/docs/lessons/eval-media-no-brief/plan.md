# One shared team calendar

slug: eval-media-no-brief · mode: online · age band: E · group: 1 · total: 30 min · version: 1 · written: 2026-10-20

## Outcome

By the end, each learner **can** set up a shared calendar that shows who is out and when, and **has made** a shared calendar for their own team with next week's absences entered. Evidence: the app checks that the calendar exists, is shared with at least two people and holds at least one entry.

Prior knowledge assumed: has added an event to a personal calendar. Prerequisite check: a one-tap question, "Have you added an event to your own calendar this month?" with a 90-second primer for "no".

### Success criteria

- criteria: the calendar has a clear team name · it is shared with the team · next week's absences are in it
- model answer or work sample: the finished calendar of the fictional Harbor Street team, shown in M1
- shown in: S2

### Expect effort

- line: "The first setup takes longer than watching it. That is normal, and it only happens once."
- said in: S3

## Story frame

- setting: the fictional Harbor Street support team on a Monday morning
- character(s): Dana, the team lead who keeps asking "who is in today?"
- stakes: two people book the same week off and the help desk is empty on Friday
- callbacks: the empty Friday returns in S4 and S6
- humour register: dry, band E, never at a learner's expense; one beat per segment at most

## Game layer

- feedback loop: the app ticks each success criterion as it detects it
- progression: three ticks unlock the "team ready" badge, awarded for the evidence only
- challenge curve: a practice calendar in S3, their own team in S4
- choice: learners choose a calendar colour and which week to fill first
- safe failure: the S3 practice calendar is deleted automatically after the lesson

### Segment detail

#### S1 Empty Friday
- instruction: "Watch the clip. Then tap the reason you think the help desk was empty."
- materials: M3 clip; three answer buttons
- prepare / what goes wrong: clip captioned / sound off on the learner's phone, captions carry it
- fast / slow / wrong: fast learners read the reveal at once / slow learners can replay the clip / wrong guesses see the reveal with no score
- transition: "Nobody could see who was out. Let's fix that."

#### S2 How sharing works
- instruction: "Tap each part of the calendar to see what it does."
- materials: M1 diagram with three tap targets
- prepare / what goes wrong: tap targets large enough for a thumb / learners skip a part, the next button stays grey until all three are opened
- fast / slow / wrong: fast learners open the "who can edit" detail / slow learners get a one-line summary per part / n/a
- transition: "Now build a practice one."

#### S3 Practice calendar
- instruction: "Create a calendar called Practice. Share it with demo@example.com. Add one absence for tomorrow."
- materials: M2 annotated screenshots; the demo account
- prepare / what goes wrong: demo account active / the share button sits in a menu, M2 callout 2 points at it
- fast / slow / wrong: fast learners add a second person / slow learners follow M2 step by step / wrong: the app shows which tick is missing
- transition: "Now do the same for your real team."

#### S4 Your team
- instruction: "Create your team's calendar. Share it with your team. Add next week's absences."
- materials: the learner's own calendar app and team list
- prepare / what goes wrong: learners without permission to share use the practice calendar and a note to ask IT / names typed wrong, the app shows the share list
- fast / slow / wrong: fast learners add a recurring team meeting / slow learners add only their own absence / wrong: the app names the missing tick
- transition: "Three quick questions."

#### S5 Three questions
- instruction: "Answer each question. You see the answer after each one."
- materials: three questions in the app
- prepare / what goes wrong: n/a / a learner misses the edit question, the explanation shows the share setting
- fast / slow / wrong: n/a / n/a / wrong answers show the right setting on a screenshot
- transition: "Your team is ready."

#### S6 Team ready
- instruction: "Here is your badge. Next lesson: handing over work before you are away."
- materials: badge screen
- prepare / what goes wrong: n/a
- fast / slow / wrong: n/a
- transition: none, lesson end

## Misconceptions

| misconception | surfaces in | how it is voiced first | the line that fixes it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sharing a calendar lets everyone change it | S5 | question 2 offers "everyone can edit" | "Sharing to see and sharing to edit are two settings." |
| Absences only matter for long holidays | S1 | one answer button says "only holidays count" | "A half day out still leaves the desk empty." |

## Check-result branch

- weak result (fewer than 2 of 3 correct): the app replays M1 and asks the missed question again
- strong result (3 of 3): the app offers the recurring meeting stretch
- recorded where: the learner's progress record

## Assignment (the practical)

- task: ask each teammate to add their own absences for the next month
- uses: next lesson starts from this calendar
- deliverable: the shared calendar with entries from at least two people
- check: the three success criteria
- time: 10 min · optional stretch: add a recurring team meeting
