# lesson house rules (eval fixture)

## Words
- language: Korean, 존댓말, warm but professional; teacher notes in English or Korean
- forbidden words: 보장, 반드시 (no absolute promises; ranges and measurements only)
- product names: never in learner-facing text; say 어시스턴트

## Audience
- band E (office workers), group 7–30 in person
- primary device: phone, 390 px wide

## Time
- live session: 50 min blocks; never more than 12 min passive

## Facts
- curriculum facts come only from `docs/curriculum/` fact sheets; anything else is `needs fact:`
- never invent statistics, time savings, prices, dates, tool behaviour

## Game layer
- points only for evidence; never for attendance or self-reported time
- no public ranking of individuals

## Loop
- bar: 8
- commit: no
