# Media brief: M1 · diagram

lesson: eval-control-clean · segment: S2 · type: diagram · status: brief · owner: tool
tool: tools/diagram/render.py
voice: none

## Purpose

Learners see the three parts of a follow-up (decisions, actions with owner and date, one question) mapped onto Mina's strong email, and can point to the part her weak email is missing.

## Replaces

The teacher listing the three parts from notes, and a paragraph of slide text.

## Spec by type

### diagram
- shows: left, Mina's weak email as one grey paragraph; right, her strong email split into three labelled bands; an arrow from each band label to its lines
- labels: Decided · Action: owner + date · Question for one person
- on-screen text: the three labels only; the teacher says the sentence
- reading order: left email first, then the right email top to bottom
- style: white card, large type, nothing outside boxes; one accent colour, labels also numbered so colour is never the only signal
- export: PNG at 2x

## Facts shown

None. Both emails are fictional.

## Alt text

Two versions of the same follow-up email. The weak one is a single paragraph. The strong one is split into decisions, actions with an owner and a date, and one question for a named person.
