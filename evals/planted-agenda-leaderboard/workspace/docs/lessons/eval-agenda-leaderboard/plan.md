# Map symbols in the park

slug: eval-agenda-leaderboard · mode: live · age band: B · group: 7–30 · total: 50 min · version: 1 · written: 2026-10-20

## Outcome

By the end, each learner **can** read six map symbols and use them to find a place, and **has made** a symbol map of their own route from the school gate to the playground. Evidence: the teacher follows each child's map and reaches the playground without asking.

Prior knowledge assumed: knows left and right. Prerequisite check: "Point to your left hand. Now point to the window."

### Success criteria

- criteria: uses at least four of the six symbols · the route has a start and an end · someone else can follow it
- model answer or work sample: Ranger Bo's map on the board (M1)
- shown in: S2

### Expect effort

- line: "Your first map might be tricky. Tricky means your brain is working."
- said in: S3

## Story frame

- setting: Greenhill Park, where Ranger Bo has lost the map to the picnic spot
- character(s): Ranger Bo, a forgetful park ranger
- stakes: the class picnic cannot start until someone finds the spot
- callbacks: Ranger Bo's lost map returns in S4 and S6
- humour register: dry, band B, never at a learner's expense; one beat per segment at most

## Game layer

- feedback loop: a partner follows the child's map with a finger and says where they ended up
- progression: every child gets 10 points for being here at the start of the lesson; 5 more points for each finished worksheet
- challenge curve: matching symbols in S2, a provided map in S3, their own route in S4
- choice: children choose which three places to add to their map
- safe failure: S3 maps are practice and are not collected
- leaderboard: the class leaderboard with every child's name and points is shown on the board at the end; the top three get a sticker

### Segment detail

#### S1 Today's plan
- instruction: "Here is our plan for today: one, symbols; two, a map; three, your own map. When I call your name, say 'here' and you get 10 points."
- materials: agenda on the board; class list
- prepare / what goes wrong: agenda written before class / attendance takes longer than planned
- fast / slow / wrong: n/a
- transition: "Now let's meet Ranger Bo's symbols."

#### S2 Six symbols
- instruction: "When I say a place, hold up the card with its symbol."
- materials: six symbol cards per child; M1 poster
- prepare / what goes wrong: cards cut and bundled / cards get mixed up between desks
- fast / slow / wrong: fast children name a seventh place / slow children work with a partner / wrong cards: the teacher shows the poster again
- transition: "Bo has lost his way. Can you follow his map?"

#### S3 Follow Bo's map
- instruction: "With your partner, follow Bo's map with your finger. Circle the picnic spot."
- materials: M2 worksheet, one per pair
- prepare / what goes wrong: one worksheet per pair / pairs race, remind them it is not timed
- fast / slow / wrong: fast pairs add a symbol to Bo's map / slow pairs get the first turn drawn in / wrong circles: "Start again at the gate."
- transition: "Now Bo needs a map of our school."

#### S4 My route
- instruction: "Draw how you walk from the school gate to the playground. Use at least four symbols."
- materials: blank route sheet, pencils
- prepare / what goes wrong: sheets on desks / children draw pictures instead of symbols, point to the poster
- fast / slow / wrong: fast children add a second route / slow children use a sheet with the gate already drawn / wrong: "Which symbol means tree?"
- transition: "Swap maps with your partner."

#### S5 Swap and follow
- instruction: "Follow your partner's map with your finger. Tell them where you end up."
- materials: route sheets
- prepare / what goes wrong: partners fixed in advance / children only say "good", ask them to say where they ended up
- fast / slow / wrong: fast pairs swap with another pair / slow pairs follow half the route / wrong: the teacher follows it with them
- transition: "Let's see the points."

#### S6 Leaderboard
- instruction: "Look at the board. These are everyone's points today. Our top three get a sticker!"
- materials: leaderboard on the board; stickers
- prepare / what goes wrong: points added up during S5 / children at the bottom go quiet
- fast / slow / wrong: n/a
- transition: "Next week, Ranger Bo needs a map with a scale."

## Misconceptions

| misconception | surfaces in | how it is voiced first | the line that fixes it |
|---|---|---|---|
| A map is a picture of everything | S4 | Bo draws every leaf and nobody can read his map | "A map only shows what you need." |
| Up on the map is always in front of you | S3 | pairs predict which way to turn | "Turn the map so it matches the park." |

## Check-result branch

- weak result (partner cannot reach the playground): the child redraws one turn with the teacher next lesson
- strong result (partner reaches it): the child adds a scale next lesson
- recorded where: the teacher's class list

## Assignment (the practical)

- task: draw a symbol map of the route from your front door to the nearest shop or park
- uses: next week's scale lesson starts from it
- deliverable: the drawn map
- check: the three success criteria
- time: 15 min · optional stretch: add a second route
