# 05 Engagement: examples

Fictional plan excerpts that show what a 4, a 7 and a 9 look like for this lens, then one good and one bad line per main check. Learner-facing lines are in Korean, as the InnovLabs house rules require. Compare, do not copy.

## 4

> slug: prompt-basics · mode: live · age band: E · total: 60 min
> | S1 | 5 | hook | 오늘의 목표 | Agenda slide: three learning objectives read aloud | see · listen | – | – | – |
> | S2 | 20 | learn | 프롬프트의 네 가지 요소 | Teacher talks through 12 slides on prompt structure | see · listen | M1 | – | – |
> | S3 | 10 | check | 퀴즈 | Kahoot, 10 questions, the top three names on the screen | do | – | points for speed, leaderboard | – |
> - S2 instruction: "질문 있으시면 손 들어 주세요."
> - Game layer: "모듈을 끝까지 들으시면 수료 배지를 드립니다."

Why a 4: the agenda is the opener, 25 passive minutes run straight into a speed-scored individual leaderboard, questions go to hands up, and a badge is promised for finishing.

## 7

> mode: online · age band: B · total: 10 min
> | S1 | 1 | hook | 뒤죽박죽 창고 | A picture of a messy warehouse and one question: "어떤 상자부터 옮겨야 할까요?" The learner taps a guess. | see · do | M1 | predict | 로봇 '모리'가 상자 더미에 끼어 있음 |
> | S2 | 2 | learn | 두 개씩 비교하기 | A short animation compares two boxes at a time | see · listen | M2 | – | – |
> | S3 | 3 | try | 상자 다섯 개 | The learner drags five boxes into order; a wrong swap flashes on their own screen with one line on why | do | – | a star per box in the right place | – |
> | S4 | 3 | apply | 내 책가방 | The learner photographs four of their own things and sorts them by size | do | M3 | badge for a correct sort of their own things | 모리가 "이제 혼자서도 하겠어요!" |
> | S5 | 1 | reward | 정리 끝 | 모리 thanks the learner; their stars are shown | see | – | – | callback to S1 |
> - The S1 guess is never shown again.

Why a 7: a situation hook, private feedback in seconds, evidence-tied mechanics and a rising curve; but the hook's guess is never answered against the reveal, and the only choice is which things to photograph.

## 9

> mode: online · age band: E · total: 15 min · story: 회의록 담당 팀, 마감 전날
> | S1 | 2 | hook | 어디가 틀렸을까요? | An AI meeting summary of three lines, one figure quietly wrong. The learner taps the line they suspect; the guess is saved. | see · do | M1 | predict, answered in S5 | – |
> - S1 instruction: "처음에는 잘 안 보이는 게 정상입니다. 듣기만 할 때보다 어렵게 느껴지도록 만든 과정입니다."
> | S3 | 4 | try | 검증 습관 세 가지 | The learner picks 2 of 4 sample summaries to check; each check gives a one-line reason on their own screen | do · read | – | XP per error found with its source line | – |
> | S4 | 5 | apply | 내 회의록 | The learner runs the checks on a summary of their own last meeting; teams race to the most errors found | do | – | team total plus a personal catch list | 팀 마감 시계 |
> | S5 | 1 | reward | 정답 공개 | The S1 summary with the wrong figure circled, beside the learner's own guess | see | M1 | – | closes S1 |
> - What the bottom team sees: "이번 주에 찾은 오류는 2건으로, 지난주보다 1건 늘었습니다."

Why a 9: the hook is a partial reveal answered against the guess, the effort line comes first, choices are small, the first wrong answer is private, and the contest is between teams inside the story, with individual feedback and a named bottom-of-board message.

## Pairs

- **The hook**
  - good: "S1: a screenshot of a reply from a client who got the wrong attachment; learners write what went wrong."
  - bad: "S1: 오늘의 학습 목표를 안내합니다."
- **Hook is a partial reveal that gets answered**
  - good: "S1 asks which line is wrong; S5 circles it beside each learner's saved guess."
  - bad: "S1 asks 'what would you do?' and no later segment comes back to it."
- **Every question names how all learners answer**
  - good: "모두 화이트보드에 번호 하나만 적어 주세요. 셋 셀 때 함께 들어 주세요."
  - bad: "아시는 분 계신가요?"
- **Expect effort, said early**
  - good: "이 문제는 배우기 전에 풀어 보는 문제라 어렵게 느껴지는 게 정상입니다."
  - bad: "S2 asks for an attempt before any teaching and says nothing about it being hard."
- **Passive stretches end in an activity change**
  - good: "After the 4-minute demo: each learner predicts the tool's output in the chat before it runs."
  - bad: "S2 learn (8 min) is followed by S3 learn (7 min) with no question between them."
- **Challenge curve**
  - good: "Try on a provided file, then the boss: the same move on the learner's own report, alone."
  - bad: "Three try segments of the same difficulty, then the lesson ends."
- **Fast feedback in try**
  - good: "Each drag snaps back with a one-line reason if the order is wrong."
  - bad: "Answers are reviewed together at the end of the session."
- **Two to four small choices**
  - good: "Pick your sample (sales or HR), pick the order of the two checks, pick the colour of your checklist."
  - bad: "One choice: pick a path, and each path earns 10 XP."
- **Safe failure, first wrong answer private**
  - good: "The first attempt is on the learner's own screen; only the pair sees it."
  - bad: "First question: the teacher calls on a learner by name in front of the room."
- **Rewards classified**
  - good: "a sticker appears when the character's problem is solved" (A, surprise tied to the story)
  - bad: "끝까지 하면 스티커를 드려요." (A, promised for finishing)
- **Mechanics tied to evidence**
  - good: "XP only for an error found with its source line quoted."
  - bad: "10 points for logging in each day."
- **Mechanics fit the band**
  - good: "G: progress shown as a path on a printed sheet, checked with the group in the first session."
  - bad: "G: a 30-second countdown on each question."
- **Structural or content**
  - good: "E: the case itself is the mystery: which of five summaries hides a changed number?"
  - bad: "E: points and levels around a slide lecture on judgement calls."
- **Lazzaro tags**
  - good: "H: the boss; E: a hidden error to find; P: team board; S: the learner's own report."
  - bad: "Every game beat is a timed quiz (H only)."
- **Chocolate-covered broccoli**
  - good: "Choosing the right source line is the move that scores."
  - bad: "Answer a vocabulary question to reload, then keep playing."
- **Competition as teams, inside the story, with individual feedback**
  - good: "Teams of four race to close the 'deadline' in the story; each learner gets a personal catch list."
  - bad: "Top-10 individual leaderboard on the projector, nothing for anyone below 10th."
- **Streak rules**
  - good: "Any of three learning actions keeps the weekly streak; one freeze per month; breaks are never mentioned."
  - bad: "하루라도 빠지면 연속 기록이 0으로 돌아갑니다."
- **Game layer changes around week 4 (course mode)**
  - good: "Week 4: teams swap roles and a new 'audit' mechanic unlocks."
  - bad: "The same points table runs unchanged for all eight weeks."
