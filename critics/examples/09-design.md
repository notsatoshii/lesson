# Examples: 09 Design

Fictional lesson: band E office workers, live with a phone follow-up, Korean house rules (존댓말, product names replaced by 어시스턴트). The topic is sorting a work inbox with an assistant. Each example quotes the material specs for one segment (slide, handout, still), because that is where this lens lives. No fact or number in them is real. Part 2 (ideas) has no anchor examples here; its shape is in the rubric.

## 4

```
M5 · slide for S2
- headline: "메일 분류"
- body: • 분류의 정의 • 분류의 중요성 • 분류 방법 3가지 • 주의사항 • 정리
- picture: 클립아트 봉투, 별, 전구 (세 가지 다른 스타일)
- type: 제목 궁서체, 본문 맑은 고딕, 강조 손글씨체
- export: SVG, 시스템 글꼴 사용
H1 · handout for S4
- "분류 기준을 자세히 설명해 보세요" ____________ (한 줄)
```

Why 4: a topic headline over a bullet wall, three type families, three icon styles, an SVG that will lose its Hangul on a phone without the font, and one line to write a paragraph.

## 7

```
M5 · slide for S2
- headline: "급한 메일은 누가 보냈는지보다 언제까지인지로 고릅니다"
- picture: M2 흐름도 (상자 4개, PNG 2x, 맑은 고딕), 라벨은 각 상자 안에
- on-screen text: 상자 라벨 네 개뿐, 설명은 강사가 말로
- icons: lucide 봉투·시계 (media list: icon family lucide)
H1 · handout for S4
- 같은 흐름도, 칸마다 "어느 칸에 넣으셨나요?" 한 줄
- "왜 그렇게 나누셨는지 적어 보세요" ____________ (한 줄)
- 우측 상단 XP 배지가 제목보다 큼
```

Why 7: one idea per slide with a sentence headline, one icon family, a Hangul-safe export and labels on the boxes; but the handout asks for a reason and gives one line, and the XP badge is louder than the headline.

## 9

```
M5 · slide for S2
- headline: "급한 메일은 누가 보냈는지보다 언제까지인지로 고릅니다"
- picture: M2 흐름도 그대로 (상자 4개, PNG 2x, 맑은 고딕), 라벨은 각 상자 안에
- on-screen text: 상자 라벨 네 개뿐, 설명은 강사가 말로
- icons: lucide 봉투·시계, 슬라이드·인쇄물·휴대폰 화면 모두 같은 선 굵기
H1 · handout for S4
- 같은 흐름도, 칸마다 메일 제목을 적을 두 줄
- "왜 그렇게 나누셨는지 적어 보세요" 아래 다섯 줄 상자
- XP는 하단 진행 막대에 작게, 본문과 같은 색
M7 · phone screen for the follow-up, 390 px
- 제목 한 줄, 흐름도는 화면 폭에 맞춘 PNG, 주요 버튼은 엄지가 닿는 아래쪽, 다크 모드 확인
```

Why 9: slide, handout and phone screen read as one set by one hand; the diagram is the brief's diagram, the type carries the hierarchy, the handout invites writing, and the game layer is felt, not seen.

## Pairs

- **Hierarchy per slide or screen:** good: "급한 메일은 누가 보냈는지보다 언제까지인지로 고릅니다". Bad: "메일 분류의 이해".
- **Type does the work:** good: two families, the headline twice the body size, a Hangul font named. Bad: bold, colour, underline and a box all on one key word.
- **One visual language:** good: the character, colours and icons match across slides, handout and phone screens. Bad: the handout uses filled icons while the slides use outline icons.
- **No slop:** good: a white card, one accent colour, the diagram. Bad: a gradient title, a stock photo of people pointing at a laptop and a drop shadow on every box.
- **Room to write:** good: a five-line box under "왜 그렇게 나누셨는지 적어 보세요". Bad: one line under the same question.
- **Phone-first for online mode:** good: the main button sits in the bottom third at 390 px. Bad: the follow-up screen needs a sideways scroll to show the diagram.
- **Game layer stays quiet:** good: XP as a thin progress bar in the body colour. Bad: a badge animation that covers the instruction for two seconds.
- **Dark mode and reduced motion:** good: the diagram PNG has a dark-theme twin and the reveal fades without motion when reduced motion is on. Bad: black arrows that vanish on a dark background.
- **Matches the media briefs:** good: the slide shows M2 exactly as briefed. Bad: the slide redraws M2 with six boxes and a different arrow style.
- **Icon family as recorded:** good: the media list says "icon family: lucide" and every material uses it. Bad: lucide on the slides and a second icon set on the handout.
- **Hangul-safe exports:** good: "PNG 2x, 맑은 고딕" in the brief. Bad: an SVG with Korean labels set in the viewer's system font.
- **On-screen text vs narration:** good: the still shows four box labels while the voice explains. Bad: the narration script printed on the slide under the captions.
- **Labels on the part:** good: "마감 있음" written inside the box it names. Bad: numbered boxes with a legend in the slide's footer.
- **Guide decks:** good: one step per slide, "보관 폴더를 누르세요" as the headline, the highlighted screenshot below, then the expected screen. Bad: steps 3 and 4 on one slide with the instruction only in the speaker notes.
