# 04 Modalities: examples

Fictional plan segments for calibration. Scenario: a band B live lesson (children 8–11, a class of 24) whose outcome is "explain how a seed becomes a plant, in order, using the words 뿌리, 싹, 잎". Learner-facing lines are in Korean per the example house rules; teacher notes in English. Nothing here is real data.

## 4

> **S2 learn (14 min):** Teacher reads slides 2-8 aloud: what a seed needs, what grows first, what grows next. Slide 1 has a sunflower photo.
> modalities (column): do · see · read · listen
> **S3 try (6 min):** One volunteer comes to the board and puts the stages in order; the class watches.
> **S4 check (5 min):** Worksheet: fill in the blanks in the paragraph from slide 8.
> accessibility: "help as needed".

Why a 4: a 14-minute read-and-listen stretch with no all-learner response, a decorative photo counted as see, a column that claims do where only one child does anything, no physical activity in band B, no alternatives.

## 7

> **S2 learn (8 min):** Teacher shows a four-frame drawing (seed, 뿌리, 싹, 잎) and says each step; the key line is written under each frame. After frame 2, every child shows on fingers which part comes next.
> **S3 try (8 min):** In pairs, children order four picture cards on their desks and say the order to their partner.
> **S4 apply (10 min):** Each child draws the four stages of a bean in their notebook and labels them.
> **S5 check (5 min):** Each child orders a new set of cards (a pea) and writes one sentence per card.
> accessibility: cards have large print; a child who cannot draw may cut and stick printed frames.

Why a 7: two channels with do for every idea, the stretch broken by an all-learner response, pictures that carry the order, hands-on in band B; gap: try, apply and check all share one ordering shape.

## 9

> **S1 hook (3 min):** Every child holds a soaked bean and a dry bean: "무엇이 다를까요? 손으로 만져 보고 짝에게 말해 주세요."
> **S2 learn (6 min):** A four-frame sequence (change over time, so frames, not text) with the key line under each; the teacher pauses after frames 2 and 3 for a whole-class finger vote.
> **S3 try (7 min):** Pairs act the stages with their bodies (crouch as a seed, reach down as 뿌리, rise as 싹, open arms as 잎), then order picture cards.
> **S4 apply (10 min):** Each child shows the bean's stages in a form they choose: a drawing, a clay model, or a recorded explanation on the class tablet.
> **S5 check (5 min):** New plant (a pea); each child orders cards and says or writes the reason for one step.
> accessibility: the spoken line is on the board; tactile cards for a child with low vision; a seated version of the S3 movement.

Why a 9: do in every row, frames chosen because the idea is a change over time, no stretch past half the cap, three practice shapes, a choice of response form, a named route for each access need.

## Pairs

- **The concept × channel grid:** good: summary lists each idea with do / see / read / listen taken from what the segments do / bad: the grid is copied from the modality column.
- **Two channels, one of them do:** good: "뿌리" is seen in a frame, said, and acted with the body / bad: "뿌리" is only read on a slide and heard.
- **Channel fits the content (Do, Show, Tell):** good: the growth order (a change over time) is shown as a frame sequence / bad: a drag-and-drop game built to teach the definition of "씨앗".
- **Longest passive stretch (house default):** good: "S2 runs 8 minutes; the band B house default is 5; the finger vote at minute 4 ends the stretch, so no finding" / bad: "S2 is 11 minutes, more than twice the cap: severity 1."
- **See is not decoration:** good: the frame shows the root growing down before the shoot grows up / bad: a sunflower photo beside the slide title.
- **Read fits the band:** good: one short sentence under each frame / bad: a 70-word paragraph on the worksheet for band B.
- **Listen has a text twin:** good: the key line the teacher says is written under the frame / bad: the order is only spoken.
- **Hands and body, bands A–C:** good: every child handles a bean and acts the stages / bad: the only do is tapping answers on a shared screen.
- **Varied practice:** good: act it out, order cards, draw or model / bad: three ordering worksheets in a row.
- **Choice of response form:** good: "그림, 찰흙, 녹음 중에서 골라 보여 주세요." / bad: every child must write a paragraph although the outcome is "explain".
- **Alternatives:** good: tactile cards for low vision, a seated version of the movement, printed frames to stick / bad: "help as needed".
- **Every learner, not one volunteer:** good: every pair orders its own cards / bad: one child orders the cards at the board while 23 watch.
