# Examples: 03 Practical

Fictional lesson: band E office workers, live, 50 minutes, Korean house rules (존댓말, product names replaced by 어시스턴트). The topic is sorting a work inbox with an assistant. Each example quotes the apply segment and the Assignment block, because that is where this lens lives. No fact or number in them is real.

## 4

```
| S5 | 8 | apply | 정리 퀴즈 | 오늘 배운 분류 기준 퀴즈 5문항 | do · read | – | 점수 | – |

## Assignment (the practical)
- task: 오늘 배운 내용을 복습하고 느낀 점을 적어 주세요
- uses: 참고용으로 보관
- deliverable: 소감 한 문단
- check: 이해도 자가 평가 (1–5점)
```

Why 4: the apply segment is a quiz on supplied material, so nothing is made on the learner's own inbox; the assignment is a reflection with a self-rating as its only check, and "keep for reference" is not a use.

## 7

```
| S5 | 15 | apply | 내 메일함 분류 규칙 | 자기 메일함 맨 위 20통에 오늘 기준을 적용해 규칙 3줄을 만듭니다 | do | M3 | 규칙 저장 시 XP | – |

## Assignment (the practical)
- task: 내일 아침 메일함에 규칙 3줄을 그대로 적용해 보고, 맞지 않는 메일이 나오면 규칙을 고칩니다
- uses: 다음 수업에서 참고
- deliverable: 규칙 3줄 메모 (휴대폰 메모 앱)
- check: 규칙대로 나눈 메일 수를 스스로 적습니다
```

Why 7: a real artifact on the learner's own inbox, usable tomorrow, and apply is long enough; but the `uses:` line names no moment, and the only check is a number the learner reports.

## 9

```
| S5 | 15 | apply | 내 메일함 분류 규칙 | 자기 메일함 맨 위 20통에 오늘 기준을 적용해 규칙 3줄을 만듭니다 | do | M3 | 규칙 저장 시 XP | – |

## Assignment (the practical)
- task: 내일 아침 첫 10분 동안 규칙 3줄로 받은 메일을 나누고, 규칙에 맞지 않았던 메일 하나를 골라 규칙을 한 줄 고칩니다
- uses: 2차시 S1에서 이 규칙 3줄을 어시스턴트 지시문으로 바꿉니다 (2차시 Materials에 '1차시 규칙 메모' 명시)
- deliverable: 고친 규칙 메모를 찍은 사진 1장 (메일 내용은 보이지 않게)
- check: 규칙이 3줄 이하인지, 줄마다 '언제'와 '어디로'가 있는지 (S2에서 미리 보여 드린 기준 그대로); 강사는 고친 한 줄을 확인합니다
- entry tier: 시간이 없으면 규칙 1줄을 메일 5통에만 적용
- time: 10분 · stretch: 같은 규칙을 팀 공용 메일함에도 적용
```

Why 9: the artifact, where it lives and the exact later moment it is used are all named; the check repeats the success criteria the learner saw before starting; an entry tier means every learner finishes some real version this week.

## Pairs

- **The artifact is named:** good: "규칙 3줄 메모, 휴대폰 메모 앱에 저장". Bad: "메일 분류에 대한 이해".
- **On their own material:** good: "자기 메일함 맨 위 20통". Bad: apply runs on the same printed sample emails as try.
- **Usable the next day:** good: the rules are applied in the first ten minutes of tomorrow morning. Bad: a filled-in worksheet that stays in the classroom.
- **The "would they do this anyway" test:** good: "an inbox you can clear before the 9 o'clock meeting". Bad: "write three sentences about why sorting matters".
- **Apply is at least as long as learn:** good: learn 10 min, apply 15 min. Bad: learn 20 min, apply 6 min.
- **Try resembles apply:** good: try uses three messy sample emails with a vague meeting invite among them. Bad: try uses three emails whose subject lines already say 긴급, 참고, 보관.
- **No toy problems where a real one was available:** good: the learner's own inbox, with a sample inbox offered only to anyone who cannot open work mail in class. Bad: a fictional company's inbox when every learner has their own on the phone in their hand.
- **The artifact is used again:** good: "2차시 S1에서 지시문으로 바꿉니다". Bad: "나중에 참고하세요".
- **Evidence, not self-report:** good: a photo of the corrected rule, checked against the success criteria. Bad: "오늘 수업이 얼마나 도움이 되었는지 1–5점으로".
- **Tool and install lessons:** good: the guide's last step sorts ten of the learner's own emails with the assistant and shows what the result should look like. Bad: the guide ends at "설치가 완료되었습니다".
- **"Real" is relative to the band:** good: band B sorts the class's lost-property list into 오늘 돌려줄 것 and 보관할 것. Bad: band B practises on a fictional office inbox.
- **Fast and slow paths keep the artifact:** good: the slow path writes one rule instead of three. Bad: the slow path switches to a matching worksheet.
- **An entry tier for the assignment:** good: "시간이 없으면 규칙 1줄, 메일 5통". Bad: the assignment exists only as "규칙 3줄, 메일 50통".
- **Gaps filled, not left:** good: a learner whose work mail is blocked in class uses the brief's sample inbox and still leaves with three rules, logged under `decisions`. Bad: S5 says "계정 권한이 확인되면 진행" and stops there.
