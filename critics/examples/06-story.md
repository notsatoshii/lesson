# 06 Story and humour: examples

Fictional plan excerpts that show what a 4, a 7 and a 9 look like for this lens, then one good and one bad line per main check. Learner-facing lines are in Korean, as the InnovLabs house rules require. Compare, do not copy.

## 4

> mode: live · age band: E · total: 50 min
> - Story frame: setting 회사; character 김 대리; stakes "자동화는 정말 중요합니다."
> | S1 | 3 | hook | 시작 | Narrator: "월요일 아침, 다들 커피 드셨죠? 저는 벌써 세 잔째입니다." Then the topic slide: "오늘은 이메일 자동화를 배웁니다." | listen · see | – | – | warm-up joke |
> | S2 | 8 | learn | 자동화 규칙 | A bullet list of the four parts of a rule. Narrator: "사실 저도 자동화는 잘 몰라요." | see · read | M1 | – | – |
> | S4 | 6 | try | 상황 퀴즈 | Three decisions about a customer email; each shows "정답입니다!" or "오답입니다." | do | – | – | 김 대리 (S1 only) |

Why a 4: the warm-up joke is unrelated, the stakes are announced, the concept sits in a list beside the story, the narrator doubts their own subject, the scenario gives only verdicts, and 김 대리 never returns.

## 7

> mode: live · age band: B · total: 40 min
> - Story frame: setting 숲속 빵집; character 너구리 제빵사 '보리'; stakes: four friends will quarrel unless 12 cookies are shared fairly
> | S1 | 4 | hook | 다툼 직전 | A picture: 보리 holding a tray, four friends frowning. "보리는 한 명에게 몇 개씩 줘야 할까요?" Each learner writes a guess. | see · do | M1 | predict | 보리: "세 개씩... 아니, 다섯 개씩?" |
> | S3 | 10 | try | 쟁반 나누기 | Pairs move counters onto four plates; the 보리 puppet asks each pair "몇 개 남았어요?" and answers what they say | do | – | team star per fair split | 보리가 남은 쿠키를 몰래 먹으려다 들킴 |
> | S5 | 6 | apply | 우리 반 간식 | Groups share 24 stickers among 6 tables | do | – | – | 보리의 사촌이 갑자기 나타나 노래를 부름 |
> | S6 | 2 | reward | 다시 평화로운 빵집 | 보리 thanks the class | see | – | – | callback to the quarrel |

Why a 7: a band-B frame whose character reacts, stays the same and needs the learners' help, with the concept (fair sharing, leftovers) in the events; but the cousin's song vanishes without loss and the reward never shows the answer against the S1 guesses.

## 9

> mode: online · age band: E · total: 20 min
> - Spine: 평소에는 회의 요약을 AI에 맡깁니다 → 그런데 어느 날 숫자가 바뀐 요약이 팀장에게 올라갑니다 → 그래서 원문과 대조하는 습관을 만듭니다 → 마침내 내 보고서의 오류를 먼저 잡습니다
> | S1 | 2 | hook | 금요일 오후 4시 | A chat screen. 팀장: "3분기 매출이 왜 12억이죠? 원문에는 21억인데요." The learner guesses where the error came in. | see · do | M1 | predict | – |
> - End of S2, just before the first try: "저도 지난주에 요약을 엉뚱한 채팅방에 붙여 넣었습니다. 여기서는 틀려도 아무 일 없습니다."
> | S3 | 5 | try | 갈림길 | Three decisions on a sample summary. Choosing "그대로 보낸다" first shows 팀장's reply ten minutes later, then the reason. | do · read | – | – | consequence, then verdict |
> | S5 | 2 | reward | 월요일 아침 | The S1 thread again, now with the learner's corrected figure and their S1 guess side by side | see | M1 | – | closes S1 |

Why a 9: the whole spine can be written from the plan, the habit is learned through what happens, decisions show consequences first, one related tool-slip beat sits just before the first try, and the close pays off the learner's own guess.

## Pairs

- **A frame exists**
  - good: "Setting: 마감 전날 사무실. Character: 학습자 본인. Stakes shown in S1: 팀장's message about a wrong figure."
  - bad: "Story frame: 이 수업은 엑셀에 관한 수업입니다."
- **Story-spine completeness**
  - good: "But one day: the summary changes a number. Until finally: the learner catches it in their own report."
  - bad: "A café setting is described in S1; nothing goes wrong and nothing is resolved."
- **The concept lives in the events**
  - good: "The leftover cookie is what 보리 tries to sneak, so the remainder is in the story."
  - bad: "S2: a story about 김 대리, then a slide listing the four rules beside it."
- **Stakes match the band**
  - good: "E: the learner's own Friday report would go out with the wrong figure."
  - bad: "E: a cartoon fox loses its acorns if the formula is wrong."
- **A–B character reacts, stays, needs help**
  - good: "보리 answers each pair's count and asks for help with the leftover."
  - bad: "A mascot appears at the top of each segment to say '다음 단계로 가 볼까요?'"
- **Scenarios show consequences before verdicts**
  - good: "Choosing 'send as is' shows the client's reply the next morning, then why."
  - bad: "Each option shows only '정답입니다!' or '오답입니다.'"
- **The story returns**
  - good: "S3 and S4 refer to the deadline, and S6 shows the S1 message thread resolved."
  - bad: "The client from S1 is never mentioned again."
- **One voice**
  - good: "Instructions, error messages and captions all use the same calm 존댓말 narrator."
  - bad: "Instruction: '파일을 업로드하십시오.' Error message: '앗! 다시 해 봐요~ ㅎㅎ'"
- **Register is dry**
  - good: "이 단계가 제일 지루합니다. 그래서 제일 자주 건너뛰고, 그래서 오류가 여기서 납니다."
  - bad: "와! 이제 정말 재미있는 부분이에요!!"
- **Every joke classified**
  - good: "Related: the summary that confidently 'rounds' 21억 to 12억."
  - bad: "Unrelated: 월요일 커피 joke before the content starts."
- **Resolvable unaided**
  - good: "B: 보리 tries to hide the leftover cookie; learners see why it is funny once they count."
  - bad: "B: a pun that needs the word 'quotient' to land, explained in the next line."
- **Narrator self-deprecation capped**
  - good: "Once per lesson: '저도 지난주에 엉뚱한 채팅방에 붙여 넣었습니다.'"
  - bad: "'솔직히 저도 엑셀은 정말 못해요.' in a lesson that teaches Excel."
- **Density and placement**
  - good: "One related beat at the end of S2, right before the first try."
  - bad: "A joke in the check segment's instructions."
- **Never at a learner's expense (severity 1)**
  - good: "The joke is on the tool that 'rounds' numbers creatively."
  - bad: "'꼭 한 분씩은 이걸 반대로 하시더라고요.'"
- **Band bans**
  - good: "B: a deadpan baker who is sure 12 cookies make 5 each."
  - bad: "B: a narrator who says '참 잘~ 하셨네요' after a wrong answer."
- **Silent read**
  - good: "A line that works read on a phone with no pause."
  - bad: "'(잠시 멈춤, 웃음 기다리기)' written into the script."
- **Native, not translated**
  - good: "세 개씩... 아니, 다섯 개씩? (보리, written in Korean first)"
  - bad: "'그것은 케이크 조각입니다!' meaning 'it's a piece of cake'."
- **The vanish test**
  - good: "Without 보리 the sharing task still runs, but the reason to share fairly is gone."
  - bad: "보리의 사촌's song: remove it and nothing is lost."
